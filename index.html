n8n Agent

n8n Agent is a privately operated personal automation integration. It is used by its owner to automate routine workflows, including generating reports and saving files to the owner's authorized Google Drive account.

This is not a public application. Access is limited to Google accounts explicitly authorized by the operator.

Google account access

The integration uses Google OAuth to access Google services within the permissions granted by the account holder. Authorization can be revoked through Google account settings.

Read the Privacy Policy for details.
