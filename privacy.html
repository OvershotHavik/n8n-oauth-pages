Privacy Policy for n8n Agent

Last updated: October 16, 2026

n8n Agent is a privately operated automation integration used by its owner. It is not offered as a public service.

Information accessed

When its operator authorizes a Google account, n8n Agent can access Google Drive information and files within the permissions granted during Google's OAuth consent process. Its workflows may create folders, create or upload reports, and read or locate files needed for the operator's automations.

How information is used

Google account data is used solely to carry out the operator's configured automations. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

Workflow configuration, credentials, and processing data are managed within the operator's privately controlled n8n environment and connected services. Google data is not sold or shared with third parties for independent marketing. Files may be stored in the operator's own Google Drive account as directed by the automation.

Security and retention

Access is limited to the operator and the services needed to run the automations. Data and execution records may be retained according to the operator's n8n and Google Drive configuration and can be deleted by the operator.

Control and revocation

The operator may revoke Google authorization through Google Account third-party connections, stop automations, or delete stored reports and workflow records.

Contact

Questions about this integration can be directed to the support contact listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
